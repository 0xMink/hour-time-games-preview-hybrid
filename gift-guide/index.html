Before you wrap it

Four things worth knowing

  • They’re physical games, not apps. A board, cards, a spinner, pieces. The only thing online is a free spinner that stands in if the real one goes missing.
  • Nothing needs batteries, and Keeper Chaos needs no reading. It’s played entirely on pictures and colors, the one to pick if you’re not sure whether the child reads yet.
  • If a piece goes missing, write to us. We’ll replace it. It’s worth saying to whoever unwraps it, because most people assume a lost spinner is the end of a game.
  • DisArmory arrives close to the day. It ships December 8, 2026, so allow about a week: safe to promise for Christmas, tight for anything earlier in December.
