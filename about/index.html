Our story

Born at our kitchen table

Hour Time Games is a family-owned game company. Every game starts as an idea at our own kitchen table, then gets brainstormed, prototyped, and play-tested over laughter-filled family get-togethers.

We believe some of life’s most cherished memories are made playing games together. Game night isn’t just fun and games — it’s a chance to unplug and truly connect with the people who matter most.

Hour Time Games was founded in 2023 by Christine Greenberg.

Games made for laughter, connection, and time together.
