Good questions

Keeper Chaos FAQ

Can children and adults play Keeper Chaos together?+

Keeper Chaos is designed for ages 5 and up, and built so the whole family can play together. No reading is required, so pre-readers can play right alongside parents and grandparents.

Where can I purchase Keeper Chaos?+

You can buy Keeper Chaos directly from our online store. Just use any orange buy button on this page. It’s also available at Amazon (opens in a new tab) and Barnes & Noble (opens in a new tab).

What comes in the Keeper Chaos box?+

Keeper Chaos includes the illustrated zoo game board, five Keeper’s Track cards, a deck of paw cards, five colored zookeeper pawns, animal tokens, and a spinner.
