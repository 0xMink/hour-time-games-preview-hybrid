Glossary of game parts

Zoo Tours entrance
The circle in the center of the board where all players start.
Animal Token
A game piece with an animal head on it. These will be collected and placed on the KEEPER’s TRACK card.
Keeper’s Track card
A game board with five different animals pictured on it. Used to keep track of the animal tokens collected towards winning the game.
Habitat
One of the nine areas of the board with animals pictured. Land on the ANIMAL SPACE for that habitat and collect the matching ANIMAL TOKEN.
Animal Space
A space featuring the animal head of the animal from that habitat. Land on it to collect the matching ANIMAL TOKEN.
Spinner
A game piece to be spun to show how many spaces a player may move each turn.
Pawn
A game piece to represent the player on the board. This is the piece moved each turn.
Paw Card
A deck of cards with various commands. Players draw a paw card whenever they land on a Paw Space.
Paw Spaces
A space with a paw print on it. A player that lands on one must draw a PAW CARD.
