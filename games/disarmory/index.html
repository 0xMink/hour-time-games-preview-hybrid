Good questions

DisArmory FAQ

How do you win DisArmory?+

Build a row of five weapons before your opponents do.

What do the action cards do?+

Action cards let you draw extra cards, swap weapons, and break an opponent’s weapon as you build your own cache.

How many people can play DisArmory?+

DisArmory is for 2–4 players, ages 12+, and plays in about 15–30 minutes.
