How many copies for a room?

One copy seats the number printed on its box, so a room of thirty needs six copies of a five-player game for everyone to be playing at once. That’s the whole calculation, done below for the usual sizes.

Rules, and what to do about lost pieces

Read the rules before you buy

The full Keeper Chaos instructions are on the site, along with the board and every piece in the box, so you can see exactly what a group would be doing before spending anything.

A spinner that can’t go missing

Classroom pieces disappear. The online spinner and paw cards stand in for the physical ones on any screen or whiteboard, free and with nothing to install. If a piece is gone for good, we’ll replace it.

Keeper Chaos is set in a zoo, and every animal on the board has a facts page, a natural stop for a group that finishes early and wants to know whether a red panda is really a panda.
