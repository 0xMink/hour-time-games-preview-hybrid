Our story

Born at our kitchen table

Hour Time Games is a family-owned game company. Every game starts as an idea at our own kitchen table, then gets brainstormed, prototyped, and play-tested over laughter-filled family get-togethers.

We believe some of life’s most cherished memories are made playing games together. Game night isn’t just fun and games — it’s a chance to unplug and truly connect with the people who matter most.

Good questions

Frequently asked

I have young kids — do you have any games suitable for a 5-year-old?+

Yes! Our Keeper Chaos game is designed with young children in mind. Its colorful and whimsical animal characters keep even the youngest captivated and excited to play. And for children as young as 4, there’s Cluck, Cluck, Moose!

Can I buy your games at the big retailers?+

Our games can all be ordered from our own online store (opens in a new tab), and most are at major retailers too. See our Where to Buy page for the game-by-game list; DisArmory is available for preorder exclusively from our online store (opens in a new tab).

What should we do if we lose a piece?+

We’re happy to replace lost pieces. Just contact us and we’ll get a replacement to you.
